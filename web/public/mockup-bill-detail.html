Elections Modernization and Voter Registration Integrity Act of 2026

Assembly bill · Carryover from 2024 · Same as SB 456 · Related: HB 89 · View on legislature ↗
Last action: Referred to the Assembly Committee on State Government — Jan 12, 2026
Sponsors: Assemblymember Sarah ChenD · Co-sponsors: Michael TorresR, Diana ReyesD, Kevin ParkD
AI Summary High relevance

This bill modernizes voter registration procedures by establishing automatic registration at state agencies, creating an online portal for address updates, and requiring counties to use HAVA-compliant equipment. It also mandates post-election audits in all 21 counties, expands early voting to 15 days, and creates criminal penalties for voter roll interference.
