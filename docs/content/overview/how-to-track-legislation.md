# How to track legislation

Tracking legislation comes down to the same five steps, whether you do it by hand or with software. Here's each step both ways.

## 1. Pick what to watch

**By hand:** Search each legislature's website by keyword, and keep searching all session, since new bills are filed throughout. For Congress, [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/) sends email alerts with a free account.

**In FloorVote:** Set your team's [keywords](/overview/what-can-it-do#set-keywords-for-the-topics-you-care-about) and AI instructions under **Settings → Config**. FloorVote checks every bill in your states, and Congress if you track it, against those keywords as it's filed.

## 2. Sort the new bills

**By hand:** Skim each new bill's title and decide whether it matters.

**In FloorVote:** Matching bills arrive already summarized and scored for relevance. Work through the [New matches](/overview/what-can-it-do#triage-new-matches) filter, giving each bill a priority or dismissing it.

## 3. Read what each bill does

**By hand:** Read the bill text, its fiscal note, and its amendments, and read them again whenever a new version is posted.

**In FloorVote:** Each bill's page puts the AI summary next to the full text, fiscal notes, and amendments. When the text changes, FloorVote analyzes the new version.

## 4. Decide where your team stands

**By hand:** Gather opinions over email or in meetings, and record the outcome somewhere.

**In FloorVote:** Your members [vote](/overview/what-can-it-do#voting) support, oppose, or neutral, and talk it through in comments on the bill. Then you [set the team's official position](/overview/what-can-it-do#set-positions).

## 5. Follow status and hearings

**By hand:** Check each bill's status page and each committee's hearing schedule, often weekly during session.

**In FloorVote:** Status changes show up in the activity feed and in daily or weekly [email digests](/overview/what-can-it-do#notifications). Hearings on your priority bills land on a [shared calendar](/overview/what-can-it-do#shared-calendar) that members can subscribe to, and a week-ahead email flags what's coming.
