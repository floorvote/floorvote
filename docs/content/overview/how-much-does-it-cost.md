# How much does it cost?

Running FloorVote needs two accounts: Cloudflare and LegiScan.

## Cloudflare — about $5/month

Cloudflare provides the hosting, email, and artificial intelligence billing. The Workers Paid plan is $5/month and includes a large usage allowance; a deployment with many users may go somewhat over. AI summarization is billed through Cloudflare too, and it's inexpensive — about $10 summarizes roughly 2,000 bills. Your AI cost scales with the number of keywords, the number of states, and how often bill text changes in what you track.

## LegiScan — free to start

LegiScan is the source of the legislative data. Its free tier goes a long way. Whether you need a paid subscription depends on how many states you track and how frequently your keywords monitor bills.
