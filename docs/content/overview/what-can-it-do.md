# What can it do?

FloorVote is for teams that track and respond to bills together. It pulls in legislation from the states and keywords your team chooses, then gives everyone one place to follow and act on it.

With FloorVote, a team can:

- Get email updates when tracked bills are introduced, change status, or move on the calendar.
- Read plain-language bill summaries generated by artificial intelligence.
- Vote on bills and record where individual members stand.
- Set the team's official position on a bill.
- Discuss bills with comments and notes.
- Share a hearing calendar so committee dates and testimony deadlines aren't missed.
