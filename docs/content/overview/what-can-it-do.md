# What can it do?

FloorVote is bill tracking for teams that want to follow legislation together, not just individually. Instead of everyone on a team monitoring bills on their own and comparing notes over email, FloorVote gives the whole team one shared place to watch, discuss, and respond to the bills that matter to them.

Here's the workflow in practice. Your team picks the states and keywords it cares about, and FloorVote pulls in the matching bills automatically. Each bill arrives with a plain-language summary, generated by artificial intelligence, so people don't have to wade through legislative text to understand what a bill actually does. From there, the team can discuss a bill with comments and notes, and each member can vote support, oppose, or neutral to record where they personally stand. Once the team has talked it through, FloorVote also lets you record the team's official position, separate from individual votes. A shared hearing calendar rounds it out, so committee dates and testimony deadlines don't slip past unnoticed.

**Who it's for:** any team that tracks a legislative issue together — advocacy groups, associations, and policy staffs alike. FloorVote was built first for state associations of local election officials, but the workflow generalizes to any group that needs to track, discuss, and act on legislation as a team.
